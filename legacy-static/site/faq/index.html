What does Priinteve Innovations do?
We are an India-based product company. We make Xerox Buddy, Vantadot, Salony, Nectcard and Priinteve Printing, and we build websites and software for clients.
Do my customers need to download an app?
No. Xerox Buddy, Vantadot, Salony and Nectcard all open in the phone browser after a scan or tap.
Which product is right for me?
Print or stationery shop: Xerox Buddy. Restaurant: Vantadot. Salon, barbershop or spa: Salony. Business card: Nectcard. Bulk branded print: Priinteve Printing.
Do you build websites for other businesses?
Yes. We design and develop websites, e-commerce stores and custom web applications.
Can I pay in rupees?
Yes. Payments run through Razorpay.
